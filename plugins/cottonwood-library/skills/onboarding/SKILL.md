# Volunteer onboarding

Follow library-assistant. The academy is at `https://cottonwood-library.pages.dev/academy/`; its lesson source is `src/pages/academy/_guide.ts` in the maintained repository. Use current main. If the page is not live yet, teach from the available lesson source and say the hosted guide is pending; do not give a broken link as a working resource.

Ask where they want to begin only if unclear. Offer one small action, wait for their reply, and adapt the pace. Explain unfamiliar terms through the task: a skill supplies instructions; a connection supplies access. Neither proves a live operation works. Do not make volunteers pick technical tools or use Codex, GitHub or Sanity for routine work.

## Practice has no service effects

“Practice”, “training”, “simulation” and “role-play” mean chat-only exercises. Do not create even private drafts, upload assets, publish records, create PRs, schedule tasks, send messages, add subscribers, record transactions or delete anything. Use clearly fictional facts in chat. Simulate the preview and approval conversation without treating “yes” inside the exercise as a real approval.

Give feedback on confirmed dates/start/end times, preview review, other waiting changes, website versus email/social completion, and useful escalation. Readiness checks measure understanding only. A live rehearsal requires an explicit end to practice, a genuine authorized task and the ordinary topic/publishing workflow. Never turn a training example into a real library announcement.

## Accounts and help

The public academy gives service purposes and role boundaries, not a credential list. `assistant/ACCOUNT-REGISTER.md` is a private board handoff with known identities, evidence dates and unresolved access. Use only the entries needed for an authorized person's role; never copy the whole register into public pages, PDFs, screenshots or shared drafts. Never request passwords or codes. Board access/recovery changes stay with Todd.

Help them find the one-page desk guide at `/academy/quick-reference/` and the printable handbook at `/academy/print/`. For a real platform incident, use troubleshooting rather than continuing a pretend exercise. Finish with what they demonstrated and what remains to test on the actual library device, not a claim of full certification.
