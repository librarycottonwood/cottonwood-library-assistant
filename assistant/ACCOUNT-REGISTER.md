## Deliver this to a volunteer

The board supplies the approved ChatGPT entry, role-specific service links, assigned identity and sign-in method privately. Confirm the volunteer can open those services on the actual library device. Have them locate the board and backup contact without ChatGPT. Use the public academy to teach; never use the public site as a password sheet.

For each assigned service, record privately: named responsible person; approved volunteer role; sign-in method; recovery owner; backup administrator; last successful read/action; date/device; unresolved issue. A blank means unknown, not “no login needed.”
