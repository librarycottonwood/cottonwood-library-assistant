## Before handing access to a volunteer

1. Give them the approved ChatGPT entry and only the other links needed for their role.
2. Confirm the account and sign-in method on the library’s actual computer.
3. Have them find the board contact and a backup contact without ChatGPT.
4. Explain the shared-computer sign-in/sign-out procedure and where access details are kept privately.

The existing shared-computer and password-manager arrangements were not established by this review. Ask the board to record its procedure; do not create an account or service to fill the gap.

For each assigned service, keep a short private note: responsible person, volunteer role, sign-in method, recovery owner, backup administrator, last successful action, date/device and any unresolved problem. A blank means **unknown**.
