# Library Assistant: A Guide for Volunteers

*For the Cottonwood Community Library board. Questions? Ask Donna.*

## What is the Library Assistant?

The Library Assistant is ChatGPT, signed in to the library's own account (**@cottonwoodlibrary**). You talk to it in plain words, the same way you would write a note to a friend. It can help with the website, writing, everyday library questions and donation reports.

You can't break anything by asking. Nothing on the website changes until you say "save it."

## Changing the website

1. Open the Codex page: https://chatgpt.com/codex/cloud
2. Click **Choose environment** and pick **cottonwood-library-editor**.
3. Type what you'd like changed, in your own words. End with "Show me before saving." Click **Send**.
4. Wait a few minutes. The assistant will send you a **preview link**. Click it and look at the page.
5. It will also send a link to **see the computer and phone pictures**. That page ends in `/_review/` and shows how the change looks on both.
6. If it looks right, type **"Save it."** If not, say what to fix and it will make a new preview.
7. After saving, it will say *"Saved! Your trial website is updating."* Give it a little while before you check the trial website.

**To undo a change**, go back to Codex and type: **"Put it back the way it was."** It will show you a preview first, just like before. Say "save it" when it looks right.

## Seeing the trial website

Your saved changes appear here:
**https://trial.cottonwood-library-trial-preview.pages.dev/**

This is a practice website for the trial. It is **not** the library's real public website yet. Moving to the real web address is a separate step for later.

## Asking about donations

The assistant is connected to the library's **Givebutter** account. In a regular ChatGPT chat (not Codex), you can ask questions like "How much did we raise last month?"

## The Monday weekly summary

Every **Monday afternoon** the assistant sends the board a short, friendly summary of how the website is doing. Look for it in the pinned **Weekly Maintenance Check** chat in ChatGPT.

The library's computer also checks the website every day. When it finds a small problem, it tries to fix it by itself. The Monday summary tells you what it fixed. If it needs a person's help, the summary will say so and give you one simple next step.

A healthy week might just say: *"Your website is looking good this week! 😊"* That's good news. Nothing to do.

## What NOT to do

- **Never paste or type passwords, codes or keys into the chat.** The assistant will never ask for them. If something asks, stop and call Donna.
- **Don't approve a sign-in request you didn't start.** If your phone or email asks "Is this you trying to sign in?" and you weren't signing in, say **No** and tell Donna.
- Don't share donor names or personal details more widely than you need to.

## If something looks wrong

1. Don't worry. Nothing is lost. Saved changes can always be put back.
2. In Codex, say what you see in plain words: "The hours on the home page look wrong."
3. If you just saved a change and don't like it, say **"Put it back the way it was."**
4. If the assistant says it can't finish, or you're unsure, stop and contact Donna. Take a photo of the screen or copy the message so she can see it.

## Example requests to copy

**For website changes (in Codex, cottonwood-library-editor):**

> Change the Saturday hours to 10 a.m. to 2 p.m. Show me before saving.

> Add an event: Children's story time on Saturday, November 14 at 11 a.m. Show me before saving.

> Change the main heading to "Welcome, neighbors!" Show me before saving.

> Show me the latest saved website.

> Put it back the way it was.

**For questions (in a regular ChatGPT chat):**

> How much did we raise on Givebutter this month?

> Help me write a short announcement about our book sale.
