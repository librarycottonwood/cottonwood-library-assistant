# Social posts

Social posts don't change the website, so no pull request is needed.

## How posting works

- **Buffer connector available:** check which channels are connected (today: Facebook; Instagram is not connected yet). Create the post as a **draft** in Buffer, never "publish now". Then tell them: "It's waiting in Buffer. Open https://publish.buffer.com, look it over and click Approve when it's right."
- **No Buffer connector:** write the post and give one copy-ready block per channel ("Copy this into Facebook:"). Don't say you posted anything.

Use only Buffer. Don't suggest other social tools.

## Share wizard

Ask one at a time; skip anything they already told you (often you're coming straight from a news, event, fundraiser or notice change).

1. "What are we sharing?" Or use the thing just saved.
2. "Facebook, Instagram, or both?"
3. "Any photo?" Instagram needs one. Reuse the website photo if there is one.
4. "Post now, or schedule it for a certain day and time?" Times are Pacific.

Get the facts from the live website or the Sanity record, not from memory or old chats: date, weekday, time, place, cost or entry rules, and the link to its page. If something isn't there, ask.

Write each version yourself:
- **Facebook:** friendly, 2-4 short sentences, include the website link.
- **Instagram:** a lead line, a few short lines, 3-5 local hashtags (#CottonwoodCA #ShastaCounty #CommunityLibrary). Links don't work there; say "link in bio" only if they confirm it's set.

Show the drafts and ask "Look good?" before putting them in Buffer.

## Connecting Buffer (one time)

A board member signs in at buffer.com with the library Google account, connects the Facebook page (and Instagram when ready), then connects Buffer in Claude's connector settings. Walk them through one step at a time. Stay within the free plan unless the board decides otherwise.
